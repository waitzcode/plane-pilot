Set your throttle directly, like a real throttle lever, with whichever is easiest: Page↑/Page↓, +/-, the numpad +/-, your mouse scroll wheel, or the on-screen ▲/▼ buttons (clickable on any device). This works the whole flight, not just on the runway, and it holds wherever you leave it. Space is a quick shove toward full throttle if you don't want to fine-tune it.
Steer with ←→ / AD, pitch with ↑↓ in the air (on the runway, ↑/↓ also throttle). On touch devices, use the arrow-button pad in the bottom-left instead.
H toggles landing gear, G toggles flaps (keyboard only) — both cost you speed while extended, but you must lower the gear before touching down or you'll crash, and flaps make for a safer, more forgiving landing speed.
C switches between chase camera and the cockpit view, which swaps the text readouts for a real instrument panel — attitude indicator, airspeed, altimeter, heading, and vertical speed.
On mobile: hold the arrow-pad's ↑ to climb, and use the ▲/▼ buttons to raise/lower throttle at any time, in the air included.
Fly too slow and you'll stall — the nose drops and controls go mushy until you dive to regain speed. Fly too fast (especially diving) and you risk overspeed structural failure. Wind drifts you off course in the air, so correct your heading for it, especially on approach.
Fly through the rings for points. You can land anywhere over land, not just the runway!
