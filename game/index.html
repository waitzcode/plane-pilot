On the runway: hold ↑/W or Space to throttle up, keep holding to lift off.
Steer with ←→ / AD, pitch with ↑↓, hold Space to boost in the air.
1-5 manually shift your throttle gear — each gear caps your top speed, so you need a high enough gear to even reach takeoff speed. It auto-upshifts if you don't touch it, but shifting yourself gets you full power instantly, and downshifting helps you brake before landing.
H toggles landing gear, G toggles flaps (keyboard only) — both cost you speed while extended, but you must lower the gear before touching down or you'll crash, and flaps make for a safer, more forgiving landing speed.
On mobile: drag the joystick up or hold the ▲ button to throttle/take off.
Fly through the rings for points. You can land anywhere over land, not just the runway!
