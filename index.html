How to Play

Pick a plane, then throttle up on the runway with ↑ / W until you lift off. Steer with ← → / A D, pitch with ↑ ↓ / W S, and hold Space to boost. On mobile, use the on-screen joystick and boost button. Fly through the glowing rings over real Manhattan streets for points, then line back up with the runway, slow down, and glide in level to land — clip a building or come in too hot and it's game over.
