SKY RUNNER

Take off from real airports and fly across real cities — New York, Tokyo, Los Angeles, Paris — over open ocean between them. A fast, free browser arcade flying game — no download, no signup.

How to Play

Pick a starting city and a plane, set your throttle, and take off down the runway. Fly through the rings for points, then land anywhere over land. Come in slow and level, with your gear down.

On phones and tablets, use the arrow pad in the bottom-left to steer and the ▲ ▼ buttons for throttle. Gear, flaps and the camera are keyboard-only for now.

Flying tips

  • Watch your speed. Fly too slowly and you'll stall: the nose drops and the controls go soft until you dive to pick up speed. Fly too fast, especially in a dive, and the airframe can fail.
  • Correct for wind. Wind pushes you off course once you're airborne. Allow for it when you line up to land.
  • Gear and flaps. Both slow you down while they're out. Lower the gear before touchdown or you'll crash. Flaps give you a safer, slower landing speed.
  • Use the cockpit view. It replaces the text readouts with a working instrument panel: attitude indicator, airspeed, altimeter, heading and vertical speed.
  • Things that end your flight: hitting a building, landing too fast, landing with the gear up, or going down in the ocean.

About

Sky Runner is a free 3D flying game that runs in your browser. There's nothing to download and no account to make.

Five real aircraft

  • Cessna 172: a light trainer, slow and forgiving
  • Boeing 737: a short- to medium-haul airliner
  • Boeing 747: the jumbo jet, heavy and long-range
  • F-15 Eagle: a twin-engine air-superiority fighter
  • F-16 Fighting Falcon: a nimble single-engine fighter

Four real cities, one world

New York, Tokyo (Haneda), Los Angeles (LAX) and Paris share one continuous map. You can take off in one city and fly across the ocean to another in a single flight. The minimap shows where you are.
