SKY RUNNER

Take off from real airports and fly across real cities — New York, Tokyo, Los Angeles, Paris — over open ocean between them. A fast, free browser arcade flying game — no download, no signup.

▶ PLAY NOW

How to Play

Pick a starting city and a plane, then throttle up on the runway with ↑ / W until you lift off. Steer with ← → / A D, pitch with ↑ ↓ / W S, and hold Space to boost. On mobile, use the on-screen joystick and boost button. All four cities share one world, so you can fly clear across the open ocean from one to another in a single flight. Fly through the glowing rings for points — you can land anywhere over land, not just the runway, as long as you come in slow and level.
